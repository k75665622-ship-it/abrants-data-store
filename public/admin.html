Storefront

Settings

These control what customers see on the storefront.

Out of stock? When a provider runs out of a network, the store pauses that network by itself (it never switches provider), leaves a message on your dashboard, and reopens it when stock is back. You can also pause or reopen one by hand here. Customers see the message below.

About 1.95% is added to the price at checkout so the fee comes out of the customer's payment, not your profit. Turn it off to absorb the fee yourself.

Clear old orders

Permanently delete delivered and refunded orders older than the period you choose. Orders that still need attention are never deleted. Your sales and profit totals will go down because they are worked out from orders, so use Export CSV on the Orders page first to keep a copy.

Delivery provider

Choose who delivers each network. For example, MTN on DataHustle and Telecel (Vodafone) on FastDealsGH. Orders already placed are not changed.
