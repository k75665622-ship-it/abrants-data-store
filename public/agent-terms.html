Agent terms

Last updated 13 November 2026

This is the agreement between you and Abrants Data Store ("we", "us") when you sell through an Abrants agent shop. Please read it properly. If anything is not clear, ask us before you sign up. We would rather answer a question now than have a misunderstanding later.

1. What being an agent means

You get your own shop on our website, with your own link. People who buy through your link pay through Paystack, and we deliver what they buy: data bundles, result checkers and MTN AFA registrations.

You are not our employee. You are an independent seller, and you decide when, where and how you sell.

Your account is for you alone. Use one account per person and do not share your password with anyone.

2. Your details

The name, phone number and MoMo details you give us must be true and must be yours. The MoMo account has to be in your own name. We check that the name on your MoMo number matches yours, and we only ever pay into that account.

If your MoMo number changes, update it in your Settings. Money to a new number may wait a day, so we can be sure the change really came from you. If you enter wrong MoMo details and money is sent there, we cannot always get it back.

We keep your details safe and use them only to run your agent account, pay you and contact you about it.

3. Prices

For each product we set an agent price. That is what the product costs you. Our own costs change from time to time, so agent prices can change too. You will always see the current agent price on your dashboard.

You choose your selling price. It cannot be lower than your agent price. Be fair with your customers: they see your price before they pay, and very high prices usually lose customers in the long run.

4. How you earn

On every order made through your link, you earn the difference between your selling price and your agent price. Paystack charges a small fee on every payment. When the store pays that fee instead of the customer, a small share of it comes off your earning. Your dashboard shows what you earn on each product as you set your price.

Your earning becomes yours when the order is delivered: the data has arrived, the checker PINs have been sent, or the AFA registration has been approved.

If an order fails or the customer is refunded, there is nothing to earn on it. If we had already paid you for that order, the same amount comes off your next payment.

Orders paid outside your shop link, for example cash or MoMo sent straight to you, are between you and your customer. We cannot see them, so we cannot pay you for them.

5. Getting paid

We pay your earnings into your MoMo. Each earning waits a short time after delivery before it can be paid (currently 24 hours), in case something goes wrong with the order.

We pay once you reach the minimum (currently GH₵20). A small fee is taken from each payment to cover the transfer cost (currently GH₵1). Your dashboard always shows the current minimum and fee, what is ready to be paid, what is on hold and everything you have been paid.

Any tax on what you earn is your responsibility.

6. How to sell

Share your link with people you know, on your WhatsApp status, in groups and on your pages. Tell customers the truth about what they are buying and how long delivery can take.

Please do not:

  • pretend to be MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo, WAEC or a member of Abrants staff;
  • promise free data, bonus data or anything else we do not offer;
  • send spam, or add people to groups without their permission;
  • use your customers' phone numbers for anything other than their orders;
  • use stolen cards or MoMo, buy through your own link to fake sales, or try any other trick to earn on orders that are not real.

7. When a customer has a problem

We handle delivery problems and refunds. Send your customer to our support line, or tell us the order reference yourself. Refunds always go back to the customer, the same way they paid.

8. Pausing or closing an account

You can stop selling at any time. If you want your account closed, tell us.

We may pause or close an agent shop if these terms are broken, if we see fraud, or if we stop the agent programme. Money you earned honestly will still be paid to you. Money from fraud or fake orders will not be paid.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When we do, your dashboard will show the new version for you to read. If you keep selling after a change, it means you accept the updated terms.

10. Talk to us

Questions about anything here? Message Abrants Data Store on WhatsApp. The number is on our website.
